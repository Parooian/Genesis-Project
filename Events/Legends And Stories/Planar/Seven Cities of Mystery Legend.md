There is a legend among Exploders and Adventurers about Seven cities. Many of them Claimed to have run across them in their travels and even brought treasures to prove it, problem is. Nobody could find them twice, they appear suddenly and disappear the same.

One appears in the Fog
One appears during Eclipses in the Wild
One Appears in the Sea
One you can wander into from a normal City
One can be found in the [[Genesis Project Repo/Events/Legends And Stories/Planar/Lantern Tract Rumor]]

Only 5 were found, but we know there are seven, nobody knows why, but everyone that arrived in one of the 5 swears there are Seven.
