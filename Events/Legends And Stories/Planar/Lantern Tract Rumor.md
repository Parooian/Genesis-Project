Travelers across the Plane say stories about a Tract that does not exist on the map. During the Night on the roads in the wilderness sometimes they see a turn unmarked on any map or not known by any person, only sign this road is not normal are the Lanterns, in every color imaginable. 

When one turns and goes through it one will always find something

Sometimes treasure.

Sometimes the Fey.

Sometimes yesterday.

Sometimes nowhere.

Sometimes their destination.

Merchants swear by them.

Priests warn against them.
