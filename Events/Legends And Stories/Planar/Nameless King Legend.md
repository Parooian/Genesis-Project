During [[Genesis Project Repo/Events/Legends And Stories/Planar/Great Fall Tale]], when civilizations were crumbling one of Monarchs of that time grew desperate to remain in power, he grew paranoid, scared and very became very easy to anger. After trying everything to soothe his paranoia he discovered a summoning ritual for one of Elder and Eldritch Gods, he made his plea and struck a deal, and thus became known as [[Nameless King|Nameless King]] Or Lich King. His people became his undead thrall and then one day they disappeared, to no one knows where, some say to one of [[Genesis Project Repo/Events/Legends And Stories/Planar/Seven Cities of Mystery Legend|Hidden Cities]] and some say they went to the Abyss.
