World of Dantar didn't always look like that, ages past it was full of great magic and beasts. Dragons Roamed Freely and Widely among humans, Giants walked the land and Arkmages and Swordmasters Were in abundance. Great Civilizations on every piece of Land. Nobody knows how, why and even exactly when but [[Folder/Game/Events/Legends And Stories/Planar/Great Fall Tale]] happened, seemingly overnight. Civilizations Crumpled and died out. Giants fell and Dragons retreated to their mountains. There are Ruins Spread all around the world with treasures and answers to questions of the past. 

We barley know anything about this time, names of some great people, name of one capital or 1 or 2 historical Events, now so twisted by time they are but a myth. After which came the dark ages of mankind, and thus [[Genesis Project Repo/Eras/Age of Ashes]] begun.
