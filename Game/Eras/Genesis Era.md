**"** *in a time before time, in a land before land, there was just a simple gaggle of gods that decided to make this plane their playground* **"** 

~ Archwizard in his thesis of Genesis Era


Genesis Era was and most likely always will be under speculation, it was before time, and before matter. Back then this plane was made of almost pure wave, closer to [[Astral Plane]] but as a plane, not a highway between them. Or maybe it was a part of it that fell off and become its own Plane, only gods and timeless beings would know. It is widely speculated and semi confirmed by a God that went to Mortal Realms to join an Orgy in their name that it was back then mostly empty and only at end's of this era inhabited by Primordials and thus starting [[Primordial Era]].
