The [[Forvaras Party|Legendary Party]] is forever remembered for their triumph over [[Nameless King|the Lich]] and his advancing army, thereby preserving the continent from ruin. Yet their victory was dearly bought, for in that final struggle they lost one of their own, [[Saintness Helena]], whose sacrifice is remembered to this day.

In the years that followed, the surviving heroes resolved that no such evil should ever again be permitted to grow unseen. Thus was the [[Adventure Guild]] established, that new heroes might be raised throughout the realm and future threats discovered before they could spread their shadow across the land.
