Many Moons ago a Comet was coming towards lands of Dantar, it would be a great Catastrophe, news spread and chaos erupted, it was a time of great despair and uncertainty, during this time, many priests gathered to plead to various Gods for salvation, many of said gods tried but to no avail, after all sky was not their domain, when hope seemed to be lost 3 goddesses whose domains were moons in the sky used their moons the innocent, they achieved their goal but during this time a piece of each of their moon got chipped away and joined together, falling somewhere over the horizon of Oceans, a legend says that now there is an [[Moon Isles|island]] somewhere made of the Moons.
