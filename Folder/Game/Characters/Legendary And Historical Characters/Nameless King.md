A greedy Monarch from dusk of [[Age of Old]]. A greedy monarch hungry for power. 

He saw that world as he knew was coming to an end, and with his paranoia and obsession he has stricken a deal with an Eldritch God. Thus becoming a lich and condemning his people to become his undead thralls. After which he retreated to one of the [[Seven Cities of Mystery Legend|Cities of Mystery]] the one found in the [[Abyss]] where he remained till he decided to try and conquer Dantar with his undead Horde.

At first his horde was triumphing with great success till his battle with [[Forvaras Party]] and his defeat, therefore marking end of [[Age of Ashes]]
